## Ungraded but Necessary to Learn: Prompt Engineering

This short course teaches best practices for prompt engineering and application development with large language models (LLMs). You will gain hands-on experience writing and iterating on prompts using the OpenAI API, and learn to build applications for summarization, inference, text transformation, expansion, and even a custom chatbot.[reference:0][reference:1]
